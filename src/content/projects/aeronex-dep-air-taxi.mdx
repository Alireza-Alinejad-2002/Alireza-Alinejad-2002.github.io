## Brief

The course RFP asked for a hybrid-electric short take-off and landing air taxi. It had to carry a pilot and 4–6 passengers, take off and land over a 50 ft obstacle within 300 ft, cruise at 150 kt or more (180 kt targeted), and use distributed electric propulsion with at least eight motors. Certification basis: FAR / CS-23. The six-person team carried the design through flap and propulsion sizing, stability, drag polar, cockpit, fuselage and systems, and the wing layout.

## Distributed electric propulsion

My first part was propulsion. Eight electric motors in the MAGiDRIVE-20 class (16 kW continuous, 20 kW peak) sit along the wing leading edge with 0.9 m five-blade propellers. They are used only for take-off and landing: their slipstream blows the wing, so the required C<sub>L,max</sub> is reached without flaps. Two wingtip Continental O-470 piston engines (225 hp each) with three-blade constant-speed propellers handle cruise. A growth version replaces them with EMRAX 268 motors fed by a turbo-generator, giving an all-electric configuration.

The S1223 high-lift airfoil came out of a weighted decision matrix that also scored NASA MS(1)-0317, NASA LS(1)-0417, FX 63-137, NACA 23018 and NACA 4412.

## Stability and control

The X-plot gave a 30 ft² horizontal tail for a static margin of about 15%, and a 31 ft² vertical tail with C<sub>nβ</sub> = 0.26 rad⁻¹. I cross-checked the derivatives in OpenVSP and against NASA's X-57 Maxwell. With one engine inoperative, the rudder deflection needed to trim is **9.36°**, well inside the 25° limit. The battery was moved from under the nose to beneath the wing to keep the CG range tight.

## Cockpit, nose and cabin

I also designed the cockpit and nose: a Garmin GWX 75 weather radar, an SR22-class instrument panel, a center stick in place of a yoke, a forward-retracting electromechanical nose gear, and pilot field-of-view checks. The cabin seats six in a 1 + 1 layout, with a 361 kg Li-ion battery under the floor.

## Drag and wing structure

The drag polar is C<sub>D</sub> = 0.0221 + 0.03745 C<sub>L</sub>² clean, which gives (L/D)<sub>max</sub> = 16.86. The wing box uses two spars, ten stringers and Al 2024-T3 ribs and skin. It was checked in ABAQUS, and isolated-wing CFD in ANSYS Fluent (k-ω SST) gave L/D ≈ 17.
